# Alarm Me

[![CI](https://github.com/MuhammadAli251018/Alarm-Me/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/MuhammadAli251018/Alarm-Me/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

A clean, no-nonsense alarm clock for Android.

## Features

- **Flexible scheduling** — repeat on chosen days of the week, or set one-off alarms on specific
  dates.
- **Custom ringtones** — pick a different ringtone per alarm.
- **Snooze your way** — configure how long a snooze lasts and how many times you can hit it.
- **Gradual volume** — let the alarm ease in instead of blasting at full volume immediately.
- **Vibration control** — turn vibration on or off per alarm.
- **App-wide preferences** — 12/24-hour time format, light/dark theme, what the volume buttons do
  while an alarm is ringing, and how long an unattended alarm keeps ringing before it times out.
